✨ Top Things to Do

  • Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke) - Europe's oldest covered bridge, built in 14th century
  • Lion Monument - Sandstone memorial for Swiss mercenaries
  • Musegg Wall - 4 climbable towers with city views
  • Lake Lucerne Cruise - Scenic boat tour
  • Swiss Transport Museum - 3,000+ exhibits, planetarium, 3D cinema
  • Mt. Pilatus - World's steepest cogwheel train
  • Mt. Rigi - Full-day hiking with stunning views
  • Mt. Titlis - 10,626 ft peak with revolving cable car

✨ Top Things to Do

  • Gorner Glacier - Second largest glacier in the Alps
  • Hiking - 400+ km of marked trails through pine forests and alpine lakes
  • Helicopter Tours - Aerial views with Air Zermatt
  • Hinterdorf Quarter - 30+ historic larch-wood houses (300-500 years old)
  • Valais Blacknose Sheep - Best viewed July-September on Gornergrat
  • Swiss Cuisine - Fondue, Rösti, Raclette, Swiss chocolate
